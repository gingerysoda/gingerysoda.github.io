Perk Privacy Policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

Perk is a personal Mac email app for Gmail. It is used only by its developer and is not offered to the public. This page explains what Perk does with Google account data.

What Perk accesses

When you sign in with Google, Perk asks for permission to read, send, and organize your Gmail (archive, label, mark read, move to trash), to read your basic profile (name and email address), and to create and read files that Perk itself creates in Google Drive (used for large attachments). Perk cannot permanently delete email.

Where your data is stored

  • Email is downloaded from Google and stored only on your Mac, inside Perk's sandboxed app container.
  • Sign-in tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain on your Mac and are never synced or sent anywhere except Google.
  • Perk has no servers. The developer does not receive, store, or have access to your email or account data.

How your data is used

  • Your data is used only to show, search, organize, and send your email inside Perk.
  • Features such as summaries and reply suggestions use Apple's on-device models. Email content is not sent to any AI service.
  • Optional Spotlight indexing stays on your Mac.
  • Perk does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind, and does not sell or share your data.

Other services Perk contacts

  • Google — Gmail, Drive, and sign-in APIs, plus Google's public favicon service to show sender icons (only the sender's domain is sent).
  • Open-Meteo — only if you use the Horizon theme, your approximate location is sent to get local weather.
  • Images in emails — remote images are blocked by default. They load only when you choose to show them.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Perk's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

You can revoke Perk's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting Perk and its app container from your Mac removes everything it stored.
